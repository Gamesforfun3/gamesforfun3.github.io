Paint a Pattern – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Paint a Pattern is a puzzle game made by Gal Samuchyan (Gamesforfun3). This page explains what information the game handles.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking. It does not send any data to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Your level progress, your settings (such as Relaxed mode) and a record of your attempts (level, time used, result) are saved only on your own device so the game can remember where you are. This data never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Children

The game collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes (for example, an online daily puzzle), this page will be updated before the change ships.
