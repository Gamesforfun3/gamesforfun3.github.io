Paint a Pattern – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Paint a Pattern is a puzzle game made by Gal Samuchyan (Gamesforfun3). This page explains what information the game handles.

In short

The game has no accounts of its own, no ads, no in-app purchases and no tracking. To make the levels fair and fun, it sends anonymous play stats: how you play the levels, under a random number made on your device. No name, no email, no contacts, no location, no device or advertising ID. You can turn the stats off at any time in Settings > About > Privacy, and delete what was sent.

Anonymous play stats (on unless you turn them off)

The game tells you about them after the first level and the first time you open Settings > About > Privacy, where the switch Send anonymous play stats turns them off. While they are on, the game sends:

  • a random number made on your device for this game only (not your device’s ID, not an advertising ID, not linked to your Apple, Google, Game Center or Play Games account);
  • the kind of device (iPhone/iPad, Android or web) and the game’s version;
  • that the game was opened for the first time, and when you start playing (so we can see how long a visit lasts and whether players come back);
  • which level order you play (gentle start or quick challenge) and when you change it;
  • for each level you play: the level number, the result (solved, wrong order, out of time, restarted, left), which try it was, the time you used and the time allowed, whether you used a hint, and the level’s size and difficulty;
  • the same for the daily puzzle, with its date and your streak count;
  • when you earn a postcard, and when you close a tip the game shows you the first time;
  • once, after the first level: when you first opened the game, picked a level order, and started and solved level 1;
  • your Relaxed mode, music, sound, vibration, auto-continue and daily reminder settings when you change them (some once per visit), and that the game was opened from the daily reminder.

A nickname (only if you want one)

After the first level you can type a nickname, or skip it; you can add, change or remove it in Settings > About > Privacy. If you set one, it is sent with the stats (2–16 letters or digits; please don’t use your real name). Without one, the stats carry no name at all.

Why

Only to improve the game: to find levels that are much harder or easier than planned, to see where players stop, and to see whether players come back. Gal reads it on a private page that only he can open, and once a week a script counts totals (for example “level 23 was solved on the first try by 40% of players”), which are used to suggest changes to the game; those totals contain no player number and no nickname. It is never sold, never used for ads, never shared with anyone else, and never combined with other data about you.

Where and how long

The results are stored by Cloudflare (our hosting provider, which processes them only for us) in a database for this game only. Cloudflare sees your internet address when the game sends results, as every server does; we do not store it. Detailed results are kept for 180 days and then removed. A short summary per player (the random number, nickname, kind of device, level reached, levels solved, days played) is kept while the game is running or until you delete it. Counts per level that contain no player number (for example “level 23 was solved on the first try 40 times”) are kept.

Switching off and deleting

Settings > About > Privacy > Send anonymous play stats: off stops all sending at once and drops anything not yet sent. Settings > About > Privacy > Delete my data removes everything sent from your device (the player summary and all detailed results), turns the stats off and starts a new random number. If you deleted the game before doing this, email us (with your nickname, if you set one) and we will remove it.

What stays on your device

Your level progress, your settings (such as Relaxed mode) and a record of your attempts are saved on your own device so the game can remember where you are. Stats waiting to be sent (when you are offline) are also kept on the device until they are sent; turning the stats off drops them.

Daily reminder

If you turn on the daily reminder (it is off until you say yes), the game asks your phone to show a short note on days when today's puzzle is still waiting. These reminders are local: they are set up by the game on your device and shown by your phone; no server sends them and nothing about them leaves your device. Your reminder choice and time stay on this device. Turn it off in Settings > Daily reminder or in your phone's settings. While the stats are on, the game also sends whether the reminder was turned on or off, like its other settings, and that the game was opened from a reminder.

Continuing on your other devices

So you can carry on where you stopped on another iPhone, iPad or Android device, your level progress and settings are also saved in your own Apple iCloud account (with Game Center sign-in) on Apple devices, or in your own Google Play Games account on Android. This happens only when you are signed in to that service on your device; otherwise everything stays on the device. It is stored by Apple or Google under your account and their privacy policies; we do not receive it, see it or store it anywhere. The game shows your Game Center or Play Games name only on your own screen. Deleting the app deletes the copy on the device; the copy in your account can be removed in your iCloud or Google Play Games settings. Your stats choice and random number are not copied to your other devices.

Children

The game asks for no real names, emails or contact details. A nickname is optional and is checked against a short list of rude words. Parents can turn the stats off or delete the data in Settings > About > Privacy, or email us.

Changes

If what the game sends ever changes, this page will be updated before the change ships.
