Paint a Pattern – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Paint a Pattern is a puzzle game made by Gal Samuchyan (Gamesforfun3). This page explains what information the game handles.

In short

The game has no accounts of its own, no ads, no in-app purchases and no tracking. It sends nothing to us unless you switch on Share my level results. If you do, it sends how you play the levels (no email, no real name, no contacts, no location) so we can make the levels fair and fun. You can switch it off or delete what was sent at any time.

Share my level results (your choice)

After the first level the game asks whether you want to share your level results and lets you pick a nickname. The switch is shown on, and you can turn it off there or tap Skip (which keeps it off). You can change it any time in Settings > About > Privacy. When it is on, the game sends:

  • a random number made on your device for this game only (not your device’s ID, not an advertising ID, not linked to your Apple, Google, Game Center or Play Games account);
  • the nickname you picked, if any (2–16 letters or digits; please don’t use your real name);
  • the kind of device (iPhone/iPad, Android or web) and the game’s version;
  • which level order you play (gentle start or quick challenge) and when you change it;
  • for each level you play: the level number, the result (solved, wrong order, out of time, restarted, left), which try it was, the time you used and the time allowed, whether you used a hint, and the level’s size and difficulty;
  • when you start playing (so we can see how long a visit lasts and whether players come back);
  • once, if you say yes after the first level: when you first opened the game, picked a level order, and started and solved level 1 (these few times stay on your device until you answer, and are forgotten if you say no);
  • your Relaxed mode, music, sound, vibration and auto-continue settings when you change them, and once per visit.

Why

Only to improve the game: to find levels that are much harder or easier than planned, to see where players stop, and to see whether players come back. Gal reads it on a private page that only he can open. It is never sold, never used for ads, never shared with anyone else, and never combined with other data about you.

Where and how long

The results are stored by Cloudflare (our hosting provider, which processes them only for us) in a database for this game only. Cloudflare sees your internet address when the game sends results, as every server does; we do not store it. Detailed results are kept for 180 days and then removed. A short summary per player (the random number, nickname, kind of device, level reached, levels solved, days played) is kept while the game is running or until you delete it. Counts per level that contain no player number (for example “level 23 was solved on the first try 40 times”) are kept.

Switching off and deleting

Settings > About > Privacy > Share my level results: off stops all sending at once and drops anything not yet sent. Settings > About > Privacy > Delete my data removes everything sent from your device (the player summary and all detailed results) and starts a new random number. If you deleted the game before doing this, email us with your nickname and we will remove it.

What stays on your device

Your level progress, your settings (such as Relaxed mode) and a record of your attempts are saved on your own device so the game can remember where you are. Results waiting to be sent (when sharing is on and you are offline) are also kept on the device until they are sent.

Daily reminder

If you turn on the daily reminder (it is off until you say yes), the game asks your phone to show a short note on days when today's puzzle is still waiting. These reminders are local: they are set up by the game on your device and shown by your phone; no server sends them and nothing about them leaves your device. Your reminder choice and time stay on this device. Turn it off in Settings > Daily reminder or in your phone's settings. If sharing is on, the game also sends whether the reminder was turned on or off, like its other settings.

Continuing on your other devices

So you can carry on where you stopped on another iPhone, iPad or Android device, your level progress and settings are also saved in your own Apple iCloud account (with Game Center sign-in) on Apple devices, or in your own Google Play Games account on Android. This happens only when you are signed in to that service on your device; otherwise everything stays on the device. It is stored by Apple or Google under your account and their privacy policies; we do not receive it, see it or store it anywhere. The game shows your Game Center or Play Games name only on your own screen. Deleting the app deletes the copy on the device; the copy in your account can be removed in your iCloud or Google Play Games settings. Your sharing choice and random number are not copied to your other devices.

Children

The game asks for no real names, emails or contact details. A nickname is optional and is checked against a short list of rude words. Parents can switch sharing off or delete the data in Settings > About > Privacy, or email us.

Changes

If what the game sends ever changes, this page will be updated before the change ships.
