Paint a Pattern – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Paint a Pattern is a puzzle game made by Gal Samuchyan (Gamesforfun3). This page explains what information the game handles.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game has no accounts of its own, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking. It does not send any data to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Your level progress, your settings (such as Relaxed mode) and a record of your attempts (level, time used, result) are saved on your own device so the game can remember where you are. The record of your attempts never leaves your device.

Continuing on your other devices

So you can carry on where you stopped on another iPhone, iPad or Android device, your level progress and settings are also saved in your own Apple iCloud account (with Game Center sign-in) on Apple devices, or in your own Google Play Games account on Android. This happens only when you are signed in to that service on your device; otherwise everything stays on the device. It is stored by Apple or Google under your account and their privacy policies; we do not receive it, see it or store it anywhere. The game shows your Game Center or Play Games name only on your own screen. Deleting the app deletes the copy on the device; the copy in your account can be removed in your iCloud or Google Play Games settings.

Children

The game collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes (for example, an online daily puzzle), this page will be updated before the change ships.
